# Data

Dataset manifests and the label taxonomy live here. Images are referenced by URL or stored outside Git.

## Field photo record (data protocol)

The data protocol is written with the partner before any field photo is collected (decision D6; first candidate partner SWaCH, Pune, decided 2026-10-02). Whatever else it says, every field photo carries:

- its consent record: informed consent, opt-in upload, no faces or identifiable people, field data co-owned by the pickers' organization (D6);
- its material class and grade labels from `taxonomy.yaml`, and who checked them;
- the site light record. The scanning rig has no lamp (decided 2026-10-02), so each photo stores either a light reading from the phone's light sensor, which faces up toward the site light when the phone lies screen up in its tray, or a gray card laid in a corner of the frame, clear of the item. The record shows which photos were taken in poor light, and it is the evidence for deciding on a lamp after the first site visit.
