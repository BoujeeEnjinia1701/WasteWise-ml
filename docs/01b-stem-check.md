# WasteWise-ml STEM check

**Verdict: open (eight rows to confirm).** WasteWise-ml's working principle, an image classifier on a phone that names the material class and grade of one item photographed on a mat, sits within known science: no row fails, and 23 of its 31 claims are within known science. Option A, the current design, has 26 rows; option B, the tray detector, has five. Eight rows stay **Plausible, to confirm**. Four rest on field numbers that only TRL 4 work can supply: the dataset size (M4), the share of items answered (M5, 66 % against 70 %), option B's hazard recall in a crowded tray (OB5) and the photo-to-result time if a full 12 MP frame is decoded (M2). Three need a measurement on bought or made parts: legibility of the flat screen for a short user (B2), the sealed mat staying flat (C3) and the shutter button's cell life (E4). One needs Amish: the picture fits the mat only for a lens of 25.76 mm equivalent or longer (S2), and lowering the lens to 420 mm would fix that but changes his 440 mm decision (N3), so it is proposed to him as D2 in WML-DEC-001. `project.yaml` records `stem_check: open`.

The first pass found four details that failed with a fix that keeps the working principle (the fourth, the phone's placement in the tray, came from the independent review), and one borderline reach. Under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 ("whenever a STEM check fails propose and implement the best fix to the design and constructability"), each fix is now in the model, BOM, build plan, precis and requirements and recorded in the design decisions register (WML-DEC-001 F1 to F3 and F5) and in WML-DDR-005:

- **F1, reach and reading (B1, B2).** The phone lies flat at 1,215 mm. For the shortest users (the 5th percentile Indian woman, 1,356 mm tall) the screen sits about 106 mm above the shoulder, so tapping it needs about 59 degrees of upper arm elevation at about one scan a minute, at the 60 degree screen of ISO 11226; and the eye is only about 54 mm above the screen, 83 degrees off its normal, where a 10 mm icon subtends about 11 arcmin against the 16 arcmin minimum of ISO 9241-303. Fix: a Bluetooth shutter button at the bench edge takes the photo, the result fills the screen in the class colour with the bin number at least 40 mm tall (about 43 arcmin for that user), and the phone says the bin number aloud by default.
- **F2, the mat (C3).** The build plan painted and varnished the mat's top face only; hardboard swells up to 25 % in thickness through unsealed faces and edges. Fix: prime, paint and varnish both faces and all four edges.
- **F3, loose cells (C1).** Cells dropped into a bare steel box could short across their terminals on the walls or on each other. Fix: tape the terminals of every loose cell before it goes in and lay it in the 50 mm sand bed clear of the walls; a tape roll lives in the box.
- **F5, the phone in the tray (S7).** The camera bump had 2 mm of slack in its 34 mm window and the phone could turn 0.72 degrees between the lips: 5.7 mm of possible picture shift against a 5.3 mm margin. Fix: a 32 mm window (1 mm slack) and the phone pushed against the far lip, which leaves 3.3 mm with 0.3 degrees of tray tilt.

Method: STANDARDS section 20, fix rule. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, results in `docs/04-calcs/stem_results.csv`; design values from `cad/src/model.py` and WML-CAL-001 (`docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`).

## Fields not relevant

Every field has at least one row. Within them, these branches are not relevant:

- Physics, fluid mechanics, acoustics and nuclear: nothing flows, the only sound is the phone's voice prompt, and nothing is radioactive.
- Chemistry, organic and environmental chemistry: the station handles waste but changes none of it chemically; disposal routes are the partner's and the municipality's (PRB).
- Biology, ecology, agronomy and epidemiology: no living material is grown or released; biological contamination of dirty items is a handling hazard (B3).

## Concept options

- **Option A, phone stand over a mat (current design):** 26 rows. Within known science after fixes F1 to F3 and F5. Open: S2 (lens, D2 to Amish), M2, M4, M5 (field or bench measurements), B2, C3, E4 (trials on bought or made parts).
- **Option B, tray detector:** five rows of its own (OB1 to OB5) plus the shared physics of the station. Within known science on optics, resolution, power and speed; OB5 (hazard recall for items at least half visible in a crowded tray) is Plausible and would need its own field evaluation. Not dropped.

## Independent review

A second agent (Claude, run headless with `claude -p`, 2026-10-25) saw only this document, WML-CAL-001, WML-PRS-001 and `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`, and was asked to refute the headline results and every "Within known science" verdict. It recomputed and could not refute S1, S4, S5, S6, S8, M1, M3 (arithmetic), M6, M7, E2, E4 (arithmetic), OB1, OB3, OB4 and the ergonomic angles of B1 and B2. It found, and this session changed:

1. **S7 (stand tilt): refuted.** A rigid stand tilting about its board barely moves the picture (0.24 mm a degree, not 15.1); the phone's own tilt (7.7 mm a degree), yaw (5.1 mm a degree) and slack in the window dominate. The first pass stacked to 5.7 mm against 5.3 mm. Fixed under the fix rule (F5) and restated.
2. **S2, PR-019 (5.3 mm margin): weakened.** The margin is 1.2 % of the depth, inside the rounding of a quoted 26 mm lens. S2 is now Plausible and open, PR-019 at risk, and lowering the lens to 420 mm is proposed to Amish (D2).
3. **M2, PR-005 (preprocessing): weakened.** Decoding a full 12 MP frame on an A53-class phone takes 100 to 300 ms, not 20 ms. Photo to result is 64 to 201 ms from a preview-size frame and up to 331 ms from a full frame; M2 is Plausible and PR-005 at risk until the app's capture path is measured.
4. **S4, PR-007 (sleeping screen): weakened.** A 0.12 W standby gives 14 % of the battery, still within 30 %. Waking the phone and opening the camera adds 0.5 to 1.5 s, so the worst case of PR-007 is 3.6 s; and whether a Bluetooth button reaches the app with the screen asleep is unproven (DEC confirm item 5).
5. **S8 against the power-bank fallback: contradiction found.** With the screen on and charging, the phone runs about 10 K over the air, so case B of PR-010 holds only below about 35 C. Stated in CAL sections 5 and 13.
6. **M5, PR-004 (coverage): corrected.** False hazard alarms leave the graded pool: 66 % answered, not 68 %.
7. **M4 (learning curve):** the design figure covers six of nine sensitivity cases, not "the central cases"; the reviewer recalls a scaling exponent nearer 0.3 for ImageNet, to check.
8. **M3:** the bound holds for the evaluation population only; hazards are to be reported by type. **M6:** stratified, clustered sampling widens the 1.8 points. **M7:** labeling only, capture and NIR ground truth excluded. Wording changed.
9. **B1, B2, B3, C1, C2, C3, E1, E2, E3, E4, S3, S5, S6, OB2, OB5:** wording narrowed (ISO 11226 is for static postures; legibility at 83 degrees needs a trial; the rig adds the stored-cell hazard; tape makes shorts much less likely, not impossible; sand gives bulk heat capacity only; sealing does not prove flatness; TrashNet's accuracy to recheck; pixels are not legibility; camera buffers come on top of the 8 MB; the button's current is an assumption; S5's check text now tests 26.7 Wh). B2, C3 and E4 are now Plausible and open.
10. **Documents:** the PRS list of requirements at risk now matches the statuses; PR-013's license review is to include the ImageNet pretraining weights (noted in WML-REVIEW).
